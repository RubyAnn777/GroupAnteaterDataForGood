## Assessment of control units (post period = from 2019 Peru / 2023 Brazil)

**Amarakaeri BZ (Peru): treated, weaker and patchier than Tambopata; not a clean control.** Evidence of enforcement: Dec 2019 joint operation in the BZ (26 ha recovered; displaced La Pampa miners); Camanti, part of the BZ, shows a drop 336 -> 105 ha post-Mercurio (MAAP #130); Nov 2021 FEMA attempt in Camanti aborted; Huepetuhe district is under the emergency decree from 7 Apr 2023 (legal coverage). Counter-evidence of weak enforcement: SkyTruth (trip Oct 2021): four ranger posts, no visible enforcement in Huepetuhe, mining open. I found no dated 2024-25 operations specific to Amarakaeri (searches surfaced none; absence in my search is not proof of absence). Reading: control got spillover (displacement in) plus some enforcement; DiD compares more vs less enforcement.

**Bahuaja-Sonene BZ (Peru): treated, intensity unclear.** FEMA operation 17 Aug 2021 (Lechemayo, Puno); a Tambopata-river (Yanahuaya, Puno) interdiction worth S/5m exists but its date was not verified. Emergency decree covers Madre de Dios districts (Tambopata, Inambari), part of whose area adjoins this BZ; the Puno portion is not in the decree. The Tambopata and Bahuaja BZs partly share the Madre de Dios setting, so it is a poor control.

**Munduruku TI (Brazil): treated in the post period.** Large operation from 10 Aug 2023 (Greenpeace; incomplete closure, mining -60% Jan-Sep 2023), then formal desintrusão from 7-9 Nov 2024. Both fall inside 2023-24. Strong in late 2024 (only ~1.5 months of the last data year), moderate in 2023. Still on the treatment side for 2024 additions.

**Kayapó TI (Brazil): lightly treated in 2023-24, strongly from May 2025.** Only one verified action: PF closing six garimpos, report of 14 Jun 2023. Greenpeace (Mar and ~Jul 2024) says Kayapó still awaiting desintrusão; new area 1,019 ha in 2023 (largest of the three), -60% in H1 2024. Formal desintrusão from 2 May 2025 (outside Brazil's Collection 10.1 data, which ends 2024; inside the Collection 11 pack series to 2025). Best control for 2023-24, but flag the June 2023 PF action and any unobserved 2024 Ibama actions (an ISA headline "26 Sep 2024 Ibama Kayapó" appeared in a search snippet; not verified).

**Gaps:** gov.br/Ibama/PF/Funai releases unreachable (election-period blackout); no SERNANP/Defensoría 2023-25 releases for Amarakaeri found; no 2019-22 Brazilian enforcement-weakening source opened beyond a search snippet on Ibama fines (2020: 9,516 vs 11,914 in 2019, Reuters via secondary); cite only after opening.
