# Comparison-area screen: was there an enforcement operation in 2017–2025?

Search date 2026-10-11. Every query is logged in `control_interventions.csv`. That includes queries with no hits, and pages opened with WebFetch, which are marked `opened=yes` with a verbatim quote. Rows marked `opened=snippet` come only from search-engine summaries. Treat them as leads, not evidence. Firecrawl calls used: 1 of 15 (the gendarmerie page returned 403 to WebFetch).

**Caveat: finding no hits does not mean there was no enforcement.** This was a desk search of online news and agency pages in Spanish, English, French and Dutch. Small Sernanp, Sernap or Fiscalía patrols are often never published, and Bolivian, Venezuelan and Surinamese sources are thinly indexed. Some regions also had enforcement at province or department level, for example Pataz's state of emergency, Ecuador's national military campaign from 2023, and Peru's national interdiction counts (729 operations in 2023). A "none found" verdict therefore means "no reported operation located". It does not mean "un-enforced".

## Which areas look like genuinely un-enforced gold-mining areas (candidate controls)

Two conditions must both hold: gold mining before 2019, and no reported operation in 2017–2025.

1. **Cordillera Azul PN, Tocache buffer zone (Peru)**: the best Peruvian candidate. Alluvial gold was documented in the buffer zone in 2013, and we found no operation in 2017–2025. It is also inside MapBiomas Peru, so it can use the same buffer-zone table and collection as Tambopata. Its pre-2019 mining area still needs checking in `peru_bufferzone_year.csv`, since it may be small.
2. **Madidi (Bolivia)**: substantial mining and documented state inaction through 2024, so a good "no enforcement" story. Snippets hint at Sernap/AJAM action, possibly in 2026, which is outside our window. The drawback is that it sits outside MapBiomas Peru and would need MapBiomas Bolivia or Amazon Mining Watch data, i.e. a different source and collection.
3. **Apolobamba (Bolivia)** and the **Kaka/Tipuani/Mapiri rivers**: no operations found, but much of the mining is semi-legal cooperative mining. This makes it a weaker analogue to illegal La Pampa, and it also needs non-Peru data.
4. **Yaigojé Apaporis (Colombia)**: no operations found, but the evidence of continuing mining is thin.

**Not usable as clean controls:**
- **Operations reported:** Nanay/Allpahuayo-Mishana, El Sira, Bahuaja-Sonene Puno side, Cenepa, Cotapata, Beni, Podocarpus, Napo, Nangaritza, Yasuní, Amacayacu/Puré, Canaima, Yapacana, Caura, Kaieteur (2017), and Parc amazonien (continuous).
- **Too little or no gold mining:** Alto Mayo, Pacaya-Samiria, Pilón Lajas, Amboró, Nukak, Chiribiquete, and Yasuní before 2024.
- **Unclear:** Río Abiseo (provincial enforcement in Pataz), Cayambe-Coca, Puinawai, Brownsberg, and Punta Brava (not identified).

**Is Opération Harpie active in French Guiana outside the Parc amazonien?** Yes. Harpie is a permanent interministerial operation that has run since 2008 and covers the whole department (1,180 gendarmes and 2,465 soldiers; Gendinfo 2024, opened in round 1). We found no source that places a Harpie operation *inside* Lucifer Dékou-Dékou or the PNR in 2017–2025. Gendarmerie activity is reported next to both: the Paul Isnard sector in 2023 and the Roura sector in 2025 (snippet). For our rule we treat both areas as **not clean**: an area exposed to a standing, department-wide enforcement regime cannot count as "no enforcement".

**Implications for the control choice**
- Most Brazilian candidates fail the rule. Twelve of 17 units had at least one mining operation in 2017–2025, and the Tapajós cluster (APA Tapajós, Amanã, Crepori, Jamanxim PN/Flona, Rio Novo, Urupadi, Alto Maués) was hit repeatedly in 2023, which is the same year as the Yanomami operation.
- The only units with **no operation found** are **Acari** and **Montanhas do Tumucumaque**. Acari also shows no evidence of garimpo, so it fails "mining before 2019". Tumucumaque's mining starts in about 2022 (Iepé), so it likely fails that rule too. Both still need to be checked against mining area data.
- **Itaituba I/II** and **Altamira** are "unclear" because operations in the Itaituba region are reported without naming units. Altamira's only dated mining operation (Dec 2016) falls just before the window. Treat them as weak candidates, and do not claim "no enforcement" for them.
- "None found" means no operation turned up in our search. It does not prove no operation took place: ICMBio actions are often published only as aggregated balances.
