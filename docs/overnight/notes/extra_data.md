## 1. INPE TerraBrasilis DETER Amazonia, class MINERACAO — VERDICT: use (Brazil monthly view)
- Source: INPE TerraBrasilis, DETER-Amazonia (Legal Amazon). WFS `https://terrabrasilis.dpi.inpe.br/geoserver/ows`, layer `deter-amz:deter_amz`, filter `classname='MINERACAO'`, GeoJSON. Layer last updated 2026-10-02 (`deter-amz:updated_date`, saved). Fetched 2026-10-10.
- Saved: `data_raw/inpe_deter/deter_amz_mineracao.geojson` (10.1 MB, sha256 3f43a5e7...f417, 8,829 polygons, view_date 2016-08-02 to 2026-09-29).
- Fields: `classname, view_date, publish_month, sensor, satellite, path_row, areamunkm (km2 of the polygon inside the municipality), areauckm (km2 inside a conservation unit), uc, municipality, mun_geocod, uf, geometry`. **There is no indigenous-territory field**, so TI totals come from a spatial join with FUNAI polygons.
- Join: FUNAI WFS `https://geoserver.funai.gov.br/geoserver/Funai/ows`, layer `Funai:tis_amazonia_legal_poligonais` (needs a browser User-Agent header; unfiltered full download returned 403, name-filtered requests work). Saved `data_raw/inpe_deter/funai_tis_selected.geojson` (Yanomami, Munduruku, Sai-Cinza, Munduruku-Taquara, Kayapo, plus Kayabi, Bau, Menkragnoti, Raposa Serra do Sol for spare controls). Polygons intersected in EPSG:5880 (Albers), area = intersection area in km2, assigned by `view_date` month. Munduruku = Munduruku + Sai-Cinza + Munduruku-Taquara (Sai-Cinza has alerts from 2020).
- Derived: `deter_mining_ti_monthly_km2.csv` (2016-08..2026-09, km2), `deter_mining_ti_annual_km2.csv` (calendar years; 2016 and 2026 partial), `deter_mining_uf_annual_km2.csv` (all Legal Amazon by state, `areamunkm`). Script: scratch `deter.py`; should be folded into `code/`.
- Annual km2 (calendar year), Yanomami / Munduruku / Kayapo: 2018 0.0 / 5.0 / 8.3; 2019 2.0 / 11.4 / 13.6; 2021 1.3 / 8.8 / 12.0; 2022 1.9 / 2.3 / 2.8; **2023 3.9** / 1.5 / 3.0; 2024 1.0 / 1.0 / 3.0; 2025 0.5 / 0.5 / 0.7. Yanomami monthly: 0.26 (Feb 2023), 1.85 (Mar), 1.31 (Apr), 0.11 (May), then about 0.
- **Check number:** the TerraBrasilis aggregated dashboard (biome Amazonia) serves `data/deter-amazon-month.json` (saved as `dashboard_deter-amazon-month_biome.json`). Its MINERACAO sum for 2019 = **105.40 km2** (sum over month x state; AP 0.23, PA 89.90, MT 8.16, AM 3.85, RR 1.96, RO 1.30). Our WFS 2019 = 105.64 km2 (AP 0.23, PA 90.11, MT 8.18, AM 3.87, RR 1.95, RO 1.31). Difference 0.2%, explained by Legal Amazon (WFS) vs Amazonia biome (dashboard). Same for 2022 (64.46 vs 64.52). The dashboard snapshot is stale for 2025 (20.13 vs 57.15 in WFS), so check on 2019-2022 only. Not an exact match; say "within 0.3%, different spatial domain".
- Value: monthly Feb-2023 event view; Munduruku/Kayapo as comparison. A surprise: Yanomami DETER alerts peak in 2023 (Mar-Apr), not 2022, whereas MAAP #242 (Yanomami: ~1,800 ha 2022 -> 330 ha 2023 -> 84 -> 45, via AMW-type detection) shows a collapse in 2023. Possible reasons: DETER dates new/expanded polygons by detection date; first cloud-free view after the rainy season; operations started Feb 2023. Report as description, do not interpret.
- Caveats: alert system, minimum mapping ~3 ha for DETER-B (not 25 ha; verify before quoting); cloud gaps; flags only new or expanded mining, not the stock, and not river dredging; magnitudes are ~10x smaller than MapBiomas additions (Yanomami ~4 km2 vs thousands of ha), a different measurement. **Never add, subtract or splice DETER with MapBiomas.** WFS content can be revised after the fact, so re-downloading later gives different numbers; the file's hash is the record. 2026 is partial (to 29 Sep).

## 2. Peru gold production by region (BCRP, source MINEM) — VERDICT: use (strong figure candidate)
- BCRP statistical database API: `https://estadisticas.bcrp.gob.pe/estadisticas/series/api/RD15556DA/json/...`. Series `RD15556DA` "Produccion de productos mineros segun departamentos: Oro - Madre de Dios (grs.f)", annual 2001-2025, source Ministerio de Energia y Minas (per BCRP metadata), updated 2026-04-30. Monthly twin `RD12991DM`. Also `PM05149AA` (national gold production, thousand kg, MINEM), `PM40097BA/98BA/99BA` (gold exports FOB musd / thousand oz / US$ per oz, 2012-2025).
- Saved in `data_raw/bcrp/`: annual JSON (3.5 KB), monthly Madre de Dios JSON (15 KB), full series metadata CSV (6.9 MB, ISO-8859-1). Derived: `bcrp_gold_annual.csv`, `bcrp_madre_de_dios_gold_monthly.csv`.
- Madre de Dios, tonnes fine gold: 2016 16.2; 2017 12.8; 2018 10.1; 2019 7.1; **2020 1.46**; 2021 1.40; 2022 1.28; 2023 1.30; 2024 1.09; 2025 1.23. National: 2019 124.7 t, 2020 85.5, 2025 130.9. Gold export value 2025: 23,313 musd.
- **Check number:** annual MdD 2020 = 1,460,224 g fine equals the sum of the 12 monthly values of `RD12991DM` (internal consistency). No separate publisher-displayed number was captured in a screenshot; the saved API output is the publisher's own number. Still to do (needs a browser): screenshot of the series page for the log.
- Value: shows registered (formal, MINEM) production falling about 80% from 2019 to 2020 and staying low while MapBiomas mining area keeps rising: reported formal output is a poor proxy for the illegal sector and tells us that enforcement moved the registered/formal supply. Also gives gold export value/price context beside `prices_annual.csv`.
- Caveats: it counts producers registered with MINEM (formal and REINFO-registered), not illegal output; level break between 2011 (22.6 t) and 2012 (12.3 t); different publisher from MapBiomas, never subtract. Description only.
