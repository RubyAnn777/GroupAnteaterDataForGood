1 · Why FZS cares

The operation we study happened in FZS's own landscape

FZS runs a program in the Bahuaja Sonene–Tambopata landscape in Madre de Dios and Puno. Among other work, it supports Peru's protected-area authority SERNANP in law-enforcement operations against alluvial gold mining in the buffer and core zones. Our question is therefore not abstract for our audience: FZS helps pay for the kind of intervention we evaluate.

10,914 km²Bahuaja-Sonene National Park
2,746 km²Tambopata National Reserve
4,500 km²their shared buffer zone. Our data: 4,506 km² for the two MapBiomas buffer zones together
112,622 hamining area in Madre de Dios, 2025 (MapBiomas)

What FZS does there

  • Remote surveillance: satellite data, overflights, drones
  • Ranger control posts and patrols
  • Support for SERNANP enforcement operations against alluvial gold mining
  • Giant otter and river turtle monitoring

Why mining matters here

Alluvial gold mining clears floodplain forest, digs up riverbeds and releases mercury. The landscape protects Andean headwaters that feed the Amazon and holds about 20% of Peru's species, including the giant otter.

What FZS needs from us

When an operation ends, what happens in the targeted area and around it? Does the money buy lasting reductions, a temporary pause, or a shift of the problem to the next river?

3 · Enforcement

Not one operation but a sequence of them

The question card treats February 2019 as the date. The record shows enforcement before, after and on top of Mercurio, plus a period when it collapsed. This shapes every comparison we make.

2017–2018

Earlier operations and interdictions

The "before" period is not enforcement-free.

February 2019

Operation Mercurio

Multisectoral police and military intervention, starting in La Pampa in the Tambopata buffer zone. Mining deforestation there fell 90%.

2020

COVID-19: fewer patrols

Police and military presence dropped; miners re-entered Tambopata.

2021

Plan Restauración

Military interventions across critical mining zones of the southern Peruvian Amazon. A second treatment wave.

7 April 2023 →

State of emergency

Six districts including Tambopata, Inambari, Laberinto and Huepetuhe; police control backed by the armed forces, renewed every 60 days.

2025

Navy leaves the Malinowski River

Control posts on the reserve's northern boundary were left without funding.

H2 2025 – Feb 2026

Mining inside the reserve

About 500 ha of new mining deforestation inside Tambopata National Reserve, ~1,000 people. Operations in Jan–Mar 2026 did not reverse it.

4 · Our question

Deterrence, displacement or delay?

Enforcement raises the expected cost of mining in the targeted area. Three things can happen, and in the targeted area alone they look identical. That is why we need comparison areas and a department-wide total.

Deterrence

Mining stops

New mining falls in the target, and the department total falls too. Best case for FZS.

Displacement

Mining moves

New mining falls in the target but rises elsewhere: Amarakaeri, the corridor, the reserve interior. The total stays flat.

Delay

Mining returns

New mining falls, then comes back when enforcement weakens (2020, 2023, 2025). The gap closes over time.

Our question, in one sentence

After Operation Mercurio, did new gold mining in the Tambopata buffer zone fall, did it move to neighbouring areas, and did it return as enforcement weakened?

5 · Stress test

What we challenged in our first plans

Before committing, we checked our first design and Ruby's conceptual draft against the data and the published record. Each row: what we assumed, what we found, what we do now.

We assumed

Amarakaeri is an untreated control.

We found

Camanti, in Amarakaeri's buffer zone, declined after Mercurio (MAAP #130). Huepetuhe and Madre de Dios districts are under the 2023 emergency.

So we

Describe the DiD as more vs less enforcement, and say both spillovers and partial treatment bias it.

We assumed

One treatment date: February 2019.

We found

Operations in 2017–18, Restauración 2021, emergency 2023, COVID gap 2020, Navy withdrawal 2025.

So we

Mark every wave in the figures; keep 2019–21 as the main "after"; read 2022–25 as a mix of return, gold price and new enforcement.

We assumed

Displacement goes to Amarakaeri.

We found

MAAP's increases were in Pariamanu, Apaylon and Chaspa, and later inside the reserve. "Rest of Madre de Dios" more than doubled after 2019.

So we

Add "rest of Madre de Dios" and the reserve interior (ANP file) as displacement destinations.

We assumed

Bahuaja-Sonene is a second control.

We found

Its buffer-zone mining (532 ha in 2025) is almost all in Puno, far from La Pampa.

So we

Drop it as a control. Mention it as part of FZS's landscape.

Draft idea

Pick controls with "no enforcement at all" via a geographic twin.

We found

Outside Madre de Dios the largest Amazon mining area is Puno with 1,938 ha (2025); inside, enforcement reached almost everywhere.

So we

Use enforcement intensity; build twins from grid cells, not departments (section 10).

Draft idea

AMW polygons show where gold and rivers are.

We found

AMW detects mining on land, not river dredges (question card 4). Annual periods start in 2018: no pre-trend for 2019.

So we

Use AMW for locations; take pre-trends from MapBiomas; select controls on pre-2019 mining only.

Draft idea

Do Peru and Brazil (Yanomami 2023).

So we

Decided: both. One design applied twice; Peru is the deeper case, Brazil the mirror image (weak enforcement 2019–22, then an operation).

Draft idea

Gold/coca relative prices as an instrument for gang activity.

6 · Strategy

A safe core, an ambitious extension

The grade rewards careful description and honest claims (30%) more than a model we cannot explain. We secure the required comparison first, then add evidence on displacement. The extension runs in parallel and only goes into the brief if it is finished and checked.

Core · required

Buffer-zone comparison

  • Tambopata vs Amarakaeri, 2014–2025
  • Four numbers + DiD
  • Displacement total for Madre de Dios
  • Placebo 2016, persistence to 2025
  • Inside the reserve (ANP file)
Robustness · optional

Event study

  • One coefficient per year, 2018 as reference
  • Enforcement waves marked
  • Drop-the-largest-unit check
  • Clear statement: 2 units, no p-values
Extension · if time allows

Spatial design

  • Grid cells or AMW polygons as units
  • Distance rings around La Pampa
  • Matched control cells
  • Fallback: describe it in brief part 4
Second case · Brazil

Yanomami: a mirror image of Mercurio

Between 2019 and 2022 federal enforcement is widely reported to have weakened (source still to find), and artisanal mining inside Brazil's indigenous territories rose from 13,704 ha (2018) to 30,980 ha (2022). In January 2023 the government declared a health emergency in the Yanomami territory; operations to remove miners started in February 2023.

  • Treated: Yanomami territory (mostly Roraima: 446 ha of mining in 2018, 4,745 ha in 2024)
  • Comparison: Munduruku and Kayapó territories
  • Added data: MapBiomas Brazil "Indigenous Territories", Collection 10.1, ends in 2024: two post-operation years
  • Measure: most mining in indigenous lands has no substance recorded; use gold + no substance

Source: question card 2. Check number: all indigenous territories, artisanal mining, 2025: 39,915 ha (MapBiomas Brazil, Collection 11).

The claim we aim for

Description New mining in the Tambopata buffer zone fell sharply after 2019 while it rose elsewhere in Madre de Dios; it returned from 2022. Cautious causal reading This pattern is consistent with the operation deterring mining locally and displacing part of it. We do not claim a point estimate of the effect.

7 · Method and process

Six steps from raw file to brief

Each step produces something concrete that the next step uses. All numbers flow from code, so the instructor can trace any one of them back to the publisher.

Describe

Compare

Two groups × two periods = four numbers, plus their difference-in-differences. We justify the control (same department, same gold belt, same size, similar mining in 2014) and the date (Mercurio, Feb 2019; 2016–18 vs 2019–21 avoids most of Restauración and the 2023 emergency).

9 · Models and assumptions

The comparison written as a regression

With one treated and one control unit the regression adds nothing beyond the figure. We write it down because it makes the assumptions explicit, and because it carries over to the spatial extension where there are many units.

Difference-in-differences

yit = αi + γt + β·(Tambopatai × Postt) + εit
yit
new mining ha, zone i, year t
αi
zone effect: size, geology, access
γt
year effect: gold price, COVID, region-wide operations
β
change in Tambopata relative to the control

Event study (the card's model)

yit = αi + γt + Σk≠2018 βk·(Tambopatai × 1[t=k]) + εit
β2014–17
pre-trends, should be near zero
β2019–21
short-run response to Mercurio
β2022–25
return, mixed with Restauración, emergency and gold price
Shaky

Parallel trends

Tambopata grew +6,400 ha 2014–18; Amarakaeri +1,200 ha.

Likely violated

No spillovers

Miners may move into the control; the effect then looks too large.

Violated

Untreated control

Camanti declined too; the effect then looks too small.

Shaky

No concurrent shock

COVID in 2020, Restauración in 2021, both region-wide but not necessarily equal.

Not met

Enough units

Two units: no meaningful standard errors. Use figure and placebos.

Fine

Consistent outcome

Same collection and class throughout. Re-mining old scars counts as new.

10 · Spatial extension

From two zones to thousands of cells

Ruby's draft points the right way: more units, controls chosen on geography, displacement measured by distance. Here is how to make it work with the data that exist.

1 · Boundaries

  • Protected areas and buffer zones: SERNANP geoportal or WDPA
  • Mining corridor and concessions: INGEMMET GEOCATMIN
  • Titled native communities

2 · Mining

  • MapBiomas Peru annual maps, 1985–2025 (pre-trends)
  • Amazon Mining Watch polygons, 2018– (locations)
  • Unit: 1 km or 0.05° grid cell × year

3 · Covariates (fixed before 2019)

  • Elevation (Copernicus DEM)
  • Distance to rivers (HydroRIVERS), to highway (OSM)
  • Alluvial geology, legal category in 2018
  • Mining before 2019

4 · Treatment and twins

  • Treated: Mercurio area, from MAAP maps
  • Rings: 0–10, 10–25, 25–50 km
  • Twins: nearest-neighbour or synthetic control on step-3 covariates
newminingct = αc + γt + β0·(Corec×Postt) + Σr βr·(Ringrc×Postt) + εct

β0 is the change in the enforced area and βr the change in each distance ring, both relative to matched twins far away. Positive βr means displacement. With many cells, standard errors can be clustered (ideally spatially).

11 · Team plan

Our answers to the seven prompts

The structure of templates/TEAM_PLAN.docx, filled in with the current design. Fill the open slots together, then copy into the template.

1

Our question, in one sentence

After Operation Mercurio (February 2019), did new gold mining in the Tambopata buffer zone fall, did it move to neighbouring areas, and did it return as enforcement weakened?

2

Unit of observation and years

One buffer zone (Tambopata, Amarakaeri) or area (rest of Madre de Dios, reserve interior) in one year, 2014–2025. Outcome: annual addition to the MapBiomas class 4.2 Minería, in hectares.

3

Comparison

We compare the Tambopata buffer zone (La Pampa, targeted) with the Amarakaeri buffer zone, 2016–18 vs 2019–21, then extend to 2025. Amarakaeri is in the same department and gold belt, of similar size and with similar mining in 2014; it was less, not un-, treated. For displacement we add the rest of Madre de Dios and the reserve interior.

4

Sources

Pack: MapBiomas Peru, Collection 4 (buffer zones, departments); World Bank Pink Sheet, Sept 2026. Added: MapBiomas Peru Collection 4 "Áreas naturales protegidas" (peru.mapbiomas.org/descargas/estadisticas). Context: MAAP #130 (2020), #193 (2023), #241 (2026).

5

Initial statistics

Our table shows 20,730 ha of mining in the Tambopata buffer zone in 2025; MapBiomas Peru shows 20,730 ha. Mean new mining fell from 1,640 to 163 ha/yr in Tambopata and rose from 289 to 574 ha/yr in Amarakaeri.

6

Main concern

Miners pushed out of La Pampa may have moved into the comparison area or elsewhere in Madre de Dios, while enforcement also reached the comparison area. COVID (2020) and later operations (2021, 2023) overlap the post-period, and with one targeted unit we cannot compute meaningful standard errors.
